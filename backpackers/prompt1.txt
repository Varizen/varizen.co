# MASTER BUILD PROMPT
## Build a Global Student Travel Earnings Platform
### Malaysia → Bangladesh Backpacker / Luggage Transport Network

Build a professional, trustworthy, mobile-first web platform that creates a legitimate opportunity for students and backpackers travelling from **Malaysia to Bangladesh** to reduce their travel cost by carrying **eligible, declared and verified personal/consignment items** within their actual airline baggage allowance.

The concept should become known as:

> **“Travel to Bangladesh and earn back part of your flight cost.”**

The platform must make this opportunity extremely easy to understand for students searching online for ways to earn money while studying or travelling in Malaysia.

However, this must **never** become a platform for smuggling, undeclared commercial goods, prohibited goods, controlled substances, or customs/airline-rule evasion.

The product must be designed around:

**Verified Traveller + Verified Item + Verified Route + Verified Baggage Capacity + Transparent Payment + Customs Compliance**

# 1. CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT

Create a two-sided marketplace:

```text
TRAVELLER
    │
    │ Has unused eligible baggage capacity
    ▼
PLATFORM
    │
    │ Matches verified demand
    ▼
SENDER / RECIPIENT
    │
    ▼

```

A student or traveller already travelling from Malaysia to Bangladesh can register their upcoming trip.

They specify:

- Departure city
- Arrival city
- Airline
- Flight date
- Ticket class
- Actual baggage allowance
- Available eligible capacity
- Destination city
- Preferred handover method

The platform then matches them with eligible, verified consignments.

# 3. 30 KG CONCEPT

The core marketing concept can be:

## “Up to 30 KG”

But never assume every traveller has 30 kg available.

The system must calculate:

```text
AIRLINE ALLOWANCE
-
TRAVELLER'S OWN BAGGAGE
-
SAFETY / OPERATIONAL BUFFER
=
ELIGIBLE PLATFORM CAPACITY
```

Example:

```text
Airline allowance: 40 KG
Traveller's own baggage: 12 KG
Operational buffer: 3 KG

Eligible platform capacity:
25 KG
```

The traveller cannot manually override the verified capacity.

The platform must show:

**Available capacity: 25 KG**

not:

**Guaranteed 30 KG**
