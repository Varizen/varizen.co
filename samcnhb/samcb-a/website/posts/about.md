**S**outh Apollo Medical College (SAMC) is the first and only private medical college in the Barishal division. After a long pause, the government has permitted only one private medical college this year (2021) to offer MBBS course at our South Apollo Medical College. The medical college and hospital were visited several times by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, the directorate of medical education, and Dhaka University. They are highly satisfied as we fulfill all requirements set up by the government to establish a private medical college and hospital. As a result this medical college is approved by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and the affiliated institute of the University of Dhaka.

**S**outh Apollo Medical College is located at Jagua, Kalijira, Barishal, within the Barishal City Corporation. It is only about 2 km from Rupatoli Bus station. The campus is large, green, pollution-free, and well-organized. The college, hospital, and students’ accommodations are on the same campus.

**M**ost importantly our faculties are highly qualified and experienced in medical teaching. We have among us the most eminent and renouned retired professor and eight ex-principals from different prestigious government medical colleges. So, the medical students of this medical college will get sufficient impetus for high-quality education and practical exposure from highly competent faculty at each level.

* **Vision:**
* To produce health professionals with essential up-to-date knowledge, skills, dedication, and professionalism to successfully continue their profession and pursue a career to better serve the nation through health promotion, prevention, treatment, and medical research.
* To provide both primary as well as speciality health care in a specific field in the community.
* To develop future health care leaders.
* **Mission:** The mission of SAMC is to turn out Competent, Compassionate, reflective, and dedicated healthcare professional who:

* Consider the care and safety of their patients their first concern.
* Establish and maintain a good relationship with patients, their attendants, and colleagues.
* They are capable of dealing with the country’s common diseases and health problems and are willing to serve the community, particularly the rural community. At the same time, they acquire a firm basis for future training, service, and research at both national and international levels.
* Are committed to keeping their knowledge and skill up-to-date through ‘Continuous Professional development all through their professional life.

* **The Hospital:**

At present, there is a 250 bedded multi-disciplinary general hospital to serve all sections of people by providing essential, and specialized health care services, as well as, practical teaching for the students. The number of hospital beds and other special services will be increased to more than 500 beds gradually.

**T**he main hospital building, with a total of 1,00,005 square feet, accommodates the outpatient department, cabin, general wards, large well-equipped OT complex, modern diagnostic lab, radiology & Imaging department, consultant chambers reception, office, and others. The hospital has its own transportation to serve patients and doctors. We provide dedicated service to patients by qualified doctors, well-trained experienced nurses, and other health workers. We have our own pharmacy on the ground floor of the hospital building. The eminent professors, consultants, surgeons of the Barishal division are available here to ensure the best services to the hospital and medical college.

**ADMISSION**

Admission to the MBBS course in public and private medical colleges is under the direct control of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. The admission test was conducted by DGME, Mohakhali, Dhaka. DGME (Director General of Medical Education) publishes the name and the merit list of eligible students each year.

* **Eligibility:**

Only the students who are listed in the merit list of DGME (obtaining a minimum 40 marks in the admission test) are eligible to apply for admission to a private medical college.
