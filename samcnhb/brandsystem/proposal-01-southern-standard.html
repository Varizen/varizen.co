01 / Brand idea

Quiet confidence. Visible standards.

This is the most academically formal direction: an ownable shield, an open book, a guiding point and a southward chevron. It borrows the discipline of collegiate systems without imitating a university crest.

Positioning

The academic leader

For students, faculty, regulators and partners who value rigorous medical education translated into responsible clinical practice.

Promise

Learning held to a higher duty

Every communication connects knowledge with character, patient safety and public service—never prestige for its own sake.

Personality

Measured, not distant

Precise typography, structured layouts and scholarly language balanced with plain Bangla and respectful human service.

Proposed tagline: “Knowledge. Character. Care.”

Symbol logic

Shield: duty and safeguarding. Open book: learning. Point: guidance and scientific inquiry. Southward chevron: place and direction. No Rod of Asclepius, red cross or borrowed crest.

Construction

Draw on a 100-unit square. Symbol: 8-unit perimeter inset. Principal strokes: 3 units; book strokes: 2.5 units. Optical corrections are permitted only in final vector artwork.

Original concept status

Inline SVG is a concept, not approved production artwork. Commission final vectors, similarity/trademark review and small-size testing before registration or fabrication.

Crest-led alternative: The supplied HD crest reads “South Apollo Medical College & Hospital.” Use that crest—not this concept shield—if the crest-led edition is selected. Legal/entity wording, rights and vector artwork remain verification gates. Review crest assets. Functional icons: Health Icons outline only.

Lockups, space and size

Clear space = B
B = open-book height. Keep all text and edges outside.

SAMCBarishal

Responsive lockup
Full name wherever space permits; SAMC only after recognition is established.

Minimum
Symbol: 8mm print / 28px digital. Full lockup: 38mm / 220px. Test before release.

Use

Primary/deep blue or one-color black on white; reversed white on deep blue; cyan only as a controlled information accent. Use hospital/department descriptors beneath the master wordmark.

Do not

Stretch, rotate, add seals/flags, redraw the book, apply shadows, place inside another crest, add third-party logos to the shield, or use warm orange text at small sizes.

Semantic use

07 / Control & rollout

Protect the Standard

Deliverables after selection

  • SVG/EPS/PDF/PNG masters; horizontal, vertical, symbol, one-color and reverse.
  • RGB/CMYK/spot color proofs; small-size and embroidery tests.
  • English/Bangla font package and license register.
  • Web tokens, CSS components, accessible documents and social templates.
  • Signage drawings, consent forms, claims register, asset log and approval workflow.

Model-specific checks

  • Does the page feel precise without becoming elitist?
  • Is warm orange restricted to accents and accessible combinations?
  • Can patients find practical actions without reading institutional prose?
  • Is every formal claim traceable and current?
  • Has the shield passed distinctiveness and legal review?

Decision gate: Choose this model if SAMC’s highest priority is long-term academic authority with disciplined institutional communications. Resolve vector originality, Bangla tagline, registered entity names, affiliations and accessibility proofs before launch.
