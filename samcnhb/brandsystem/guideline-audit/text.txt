P6 [Normal] 00 / IDENTITY
P7 [Normal] The crest is the institution
P8 [Normal] The supplied crest combines the institutional name, medical emblem, red/blue field, stars, ESTD: 2021 and SAMC ribbon into one formal identity.
P12 [Normal] 01 / CREST ANATOMY
P13 [Normal] Preserve the artwork
P14 [Normal] The crest is a seal. Its individual elements may be explained, but they must not be rearranged, redrawn or independently stylized.
P18 [Normal] 02 / LOGO USE
P19 [Normal] Clear, formal and consistent
P21 [List Bullet] Use the full-color crest on white or very light backgrounds whenever possible.
P22 [List Bullet] Maintain clear space around the outermost visible point of the crest.
P23 [List Bullet] Create separate approved print, digital, monochrome and reverse variants from the master asset.
P24 [List Bullet] Establish minimum sizes by application: print, signage, website, app icon and embroidery.
P25 [List Bullet] Never stretch, compress, rotate, shadow, bevel, outline or recolor the crest.
P26 [List Bullet] Never place the full crest over a busy photograph where its lettering becomes unreadable.
P27 [List Bullet] Do not use the crest as a repeating decorative pattern.
P28 [Normal] 03 / COLOR
P29 [Normal] Crest-derived institutional palette
P30 [Normal] The crest is multicolor, but external brand applications should remain disciplined. One site or campaign should use one controlled working set.
P34 [Normal] 04 / TYPOGRAPHY
P35 [Normal] Academic authority + modern usability
P36 [Normal] The crest already carries a traditional academic character. Supporting typography should therefore be clean and restrained.
P40 [Normal] 05 / BILINGUAL
P41 [Normal] English + বাংলা are equal states
P42 [Normal] Every public-facing digital experience should be designed as two complete language states.
P44 [List Bullet] Persistent EN / বাংলা switch.
P45 [List Bullet] Every core English page has a corresponding Bangla page and vice versa.
P46 [List Bullet] Recommended /en/... and /bn/... structure.
P47 [List Bullet] Navigation, buttons, forms, validation, errors, confirmations and help are bilingual.
P48 [List Bullet] Patient-facing information uses plain, natural Bangla.
P49 [List Bullet] Accepted medical English may appear in parentheses when clinically useful.
P50 [List Bullet] Bangla is LTR and receives independent responsive QA.
P51 [List Bullet] Literal machine translation is not a final publishing standard.
P52 [List Bullet] Missing critical translation is a release blocker.
P54 [Normal] 06 / INSTITUTIONAL ARCHITECTURE
P55 [Normal] One SAMC · two core divisions
P56 [Normal] The visual identity should mirror the institution's operating model: medical care and medical professional education under one master SAMC identity.
P60 [Normal] 07 / PHOTOGRAPHY
P61 [Normal] HD imagery is a core brand asset
P62 [Normal] The institution should look real, credible and human—not like a generic healthcare startup.
P66 [Normal] 08 / 77 CREATIVE LIBRARY
P67 [Normal] One master identity, 77 production surfaces
P68 [Normal] Every creative type inherits the actual crest, institutional palette, bilingual standard and one-site/one-set discipline.
P71 [Normal] 09 / DIGITAL
P72 [Normal] Website behavior
P73 [Normal] The website may be contemporary and spacious, but the actual SAMC crest remains the anchor.
P76 [Normal] 10 / GOVERNANCE
P77 [Normal] Protect the master identity
P78 [Normal] A crest-led institution needs one authoritative source of truth.
P81 [Normal] 11 / RELEASE CHECKLIST
P82 [Normal] Nothing ships until it passes
P84 [List Bullet] Correct approved SAMC crest used.
P85 [List Bullet] Correct institutional/legal name used where required.
P86 [List Bullet] No alteration of crest artwork.
P87 [List Bullet] One controlled color set used outside the crest.
P88 [List Bullet] English and Bangla content complete.
P89 [List Bullet] Bangla rendering tested on target devices.
P90 [List Bullet] HD photography approved, licensed and appropriately cropped.
P91 [List Bullet] Patient privacy and dignity protected.
P92 [List Bullet] Accessibility and contrast checked.
P93 [List Bullet] Responsive EN/BN states tested.
P94 [List Bullet] Source files and exports archived.
P95 [List Bullet] Version and approval status recorded.
