01 / Benchmark study

National and international patterns

The strongest academic health brands do not rely on decoration. They make the institution's role clear, preserve trust, and connect education, research and patient care.

Bangladesh & regional comparators

  • South Apollo Medical College — own baselinePublic mission emphasizes competent, compassionate professionals; patient safety; rural community service; continued learning. Campus, hospital and accommodation share a green Barishal campus. Website affiliation and approval claims require current documentary verification before publication.
  • Popular Medical College Hospital, DhakaObservable priorities: doctors, services, appointments, indoor/outdoor care, rate charts, news and careers. Lesson: patient actions and service information must be easy to find; brand communication should not stop at institutional history.
  • Sher-e-Bangla Medical College, BarishalSelected as a regional academic comparator. The text extractor could not retrieve meaningful page content, so no visual or positioning claim is inferred. Direct stakeholder research remains necessary.
  • Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical UniversitySelected as a national medical-university comparator. Text extraction was unavailable; no unverified identity attributes are claimed.
  • Apollo Hospitals, India — name-similarity comparator onlyObservable architecture connects clinical services, patient journeys, doctors, locations, academics, research and corporate responsibility. Its messaging links excellence and compassion. Lesson: an ownable SAMCB identity is essential; no affiliation, endorsement or visual borrowing is implied.

International academic-health comparators

  • Johns Hopkins University — official brand systemOfficial guidance is organized into visual identity, application, messaging, resources and governance. Lesson: protect the master brand with templates, asset control, training and a clear approval owner.
  • Johns Hopkins MedicinePublic communication connects patient care, school of medicine and research, with prominent actions and language assistance. Lesson: one ecosystem can serve different audiences without fragmenting its identity.
  • Mayo Clinic — research and educationEducation and research share a mission with care; explicit audience pathways serve students, professionals, researchers, staff and media. Lesson: build around mission and user needs rather than internal org charts alone.
  • Harvard University — official identity resourceSelected for institutional identity governance. Page extraction was unavailable; no unverified logo or color specifications are copied.

Research boundary: This is a focused public-source benchmark, not a market-research survey or trademark clearance. No competitor marks, slogans, proprietary graphics or exact palettes are reused.

02 / Non-negotiable strategy

One master brand. Distinct expressions.

Institution

Official English: South Apollo Medical College

Contextual descriptor: Barishal, Bangladesh

Short name: SAMC

Digital shorthand: SAMCB, where disambiguation is useful.

Clinical entity

Use the verified legal and approved operational name, e.g. South Apollo Medical College Hospital, only after leadership confirms the registered wording.

Do not improvise a hospital logo or abbreviate inconsistently.

Purpose

Prepare competent, compassionate, reflective health professionals who put patient care and safety first, serve communities and continue learning.

Brand idea: Learning that becomes care.

Patients & families

Clarity, dignity, safe care, directions, appointments, prices where approved, language support and privacy.

Students & applicants

Credible education, authentic campus life, verified admissions, faculty, facilities and policies.

Faculty & staff

Belonging, professional standards, usable templates, recognition and coherent internal communication.

Partners & regulators

Evidence, traceable approvals, responsible claims, research, governance and regional impact.

04 / Shared operating standards

Air-tight by process, not just appearance

Voice & evidence

  • Clear: short sentences; meaningful headings; explain technical language.
  • Human: respectful, calm and inclusive; never exploit illness or fear.
  • Credible: source and date approvals, rankings, statistics and outcomes; never imply guaranteed results.
  • Bilingual: English and Bangla are equal communication channels, with native review and matching meaning.
  • Accountable: content owner, reviewer, approval date and review date for every high-risk page.

Visual & accessible

  • WCAG 2.2 AA target: 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text and meaningful UI components.
  • Never use color alone for emergency, department or status information.
  • Minimum 16px digital body text; 44×44 CSS px recommended touch targets.
  • Real photography only with consent and permission; no identifiable patient information without explicit authorization.
  • SVG for screen/large print, production PDF/X for vendors, PNG for office use, monochrome for single-color reproduction.

Always

Use verified names and current contacts; write “Affiliated with…” only with current evidence; preserve master-brand hierarchy; document consent; date time-sensitive claims.

Never

Claim to be best, guaranteed, internationally accredited or Apollo-affiliated without valid evidence; use red crosses or protected emblems; copy another institution’s identity; use AI-generated patient/campus images as documentary evidence.

05 / Production & governance

One owner. Controlled versions.

Approval matrix

Routine noticesContent owner + Communications template check.
Admissions / feesPrincipal/authorized academic owner + current regulatory evidence.
Clinical claimsAuthorized clinical reviewer + Communications + date.
Logo / partnershipBrand owner + leadership + third-party permission.
Crisis / patient storyDesignated spokesperson + privacy/consent + leadership.
New sub-brandBrand council; legal, longevity and architecture review.

Asset register

Every approved asset receives: model/version, file name, owner, date, permitted use, color space, source file, license/consent status and replacement history.

Proposed convention: SAMC_[Model]_[Asset]_[Language]_[Color]_[Version]

Only the published master folder is used. Concept HTML files and their inline SVGs are not approved production artwork.

06 / Measurement

Measure recognition and service

Recognition

Unaided/aided awareness, correct-name recall, logo recognition, Barishal stakeholder baseline then annual survey.

Trust

Message credibility, patient comprehension, consent compliance, corrections and claim-evidence completeness.

Service

Task completion for appointments/admissions, accessible content, response times, wayfinding success and language use.

Consistency

Template adoption, approved-asset usage, unauthorized logo incidents, review completion and staff training.
