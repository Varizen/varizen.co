01 / Choose a direction

Three genuinely different systems

01 / Trust · Care · Academic Excellence

Blue Professional / Institutional Care

Professional, reliable and clean. Deep blue, cyan information accents, structured typography and a scholarly shield.

  • Best for: institutional trust and academic communication.
  • Type pairing: Muller + Hind Siliguri.
  • Watch: warmth and proof behind formality.

02 / Growth · Wellbeing · Community Health

Green Wellness / One Campus

Fresh, balanced and human. Green/teal pathways connect education, patient care and community wellbeing.

  • Best for: college–hospital coherence and community health.
  • Type pairing: Panton + Hind Siliguri.
  • Watch: substantiate wellbeing and environmental claims.
Open complete proposal
03 / Innovation · Technology · Global Impact

Purple Innovation / Future-Ready Learning

Modern, distinctive and knowledge-led. Purple/indigo digital experiences balanced with human teal/orange accents.

  • Best for: future-ready learning, research and digital experiences.
  • Type pairing: Nexa Pro + Anek Bangla.
  • Watch: never claim technology or global outcomes without evidence.

04 / After selection

Decision first. Production second.

GATE 01

Select

Choose one direction. Do not mix blue, green and purple palettes or all font candidates.

GATE 02

Verify

Resolve South Asian/South Apollo, trademark, rights, Bangla, affiliations and service claims.

GATE 03

Produce

Final vectors, licensed English font, accessible tokens, templates, signage and asset register.

GATE 04

Govern

Brand owner, staff training, evidence/consent controls, content review and annual audit.

Recommendation, not a decision: Blue Professional is the safest institutional default. Green Wellness is strongest for connected community care. Purple Innovation is strongest only if SAMC can demonstrate a real digital/research strategy. Stakeholder testing should decide.
