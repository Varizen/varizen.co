01 / Brand idea

The space between learning and care

Two continuous paths meet at a shared point: knowledge becomes service. This direction makes the college–hospital relationship understandable and puts practical patient and student journeys at the center.

Positioning

The connected academic-health campus

A single ecosystem for medical students, teachers, clinicians, patients and community partners—not separate institutional silos.

Promise

Every lesson has a human outcome

Education and care reinforce one another through safe, respectful, accountable practice and accessible information.

Personality

Capable and approachable

Simple sans-serif typography, confident teal, generous rounded geometry, direct actions and bilingual wayfinding.

Proposed tagline: “Growing learning. Growing wellbeing.”

02 / Signature identity

The Connection symbol

South Apollo
Medical CollegeCollege · Hospital · Barishal
SAMCGrowing learning. Growing wellbeing.

Symbol logic

Two open paths: learning and care. Meeting line: supervised clinical practice. Point: the person/community served. Open arcs indicate exchange, not a closed institutional boundary.

Construction

100-unit square; main path 6 units; centerline at 50 units; terminal point 6 units radius; minimum 10 units clear between paths. The abstract symbol must not be read as a monitor or device.

Distinct by structure

No shield, serif crest or botanical emblem. Rounded terminals and asymmetric open geometry distinguish this from the other two proposals. Final vector and trademark review remain required.

Crest-led alternative: The supplied HD crest reads “South Apollo Medical College & Hospital.” Use that crest—not this concept connection mark—if the crest-led edition is selected. Legal/entity wording, rights and vector artwork remain verification gates. Review crest assets. Functional icons: Health Icons outline only.

Lockups, space and size

Clear space = P
P = terminal-point diameter, including its full height on every side.

SAMCOne campus

Digital lockup
SAMC for small UI; full name at first encounter and formal contexts.

Minimum
Symbol: 9mm / 30px. Full lockup: 42mm / 240px. Test embroidered and favicon variants.

Use

Teal on white, white on primary green, or single-color black. College and Hospital descriptors use identical symbol proportions and spacing. Use solid backgrounds and explicit text labels.

Do not

Close the paths into a generic circle, turn the point into a heart/cross, introduce gradients into the mark, use separate hospital symbols, or animate paths around patient information.

04 / Graphic & editorial language

Connected pathways, not decoration

Connection field: 28-unit dot grid linking journey steps and diagram nodes. Use at 10% tint on non-critical surfaces; never mimic test results or medical waveforms.

PEOPLE + PRACTICE

Show the exchange of knowledge.

Concept placeholder—not a documentary image.

Photography

Two-frame storytelling: educator/student interaction followed by respectful care or community impact. Honest environmental detail, inclusive representation, no artificial doctor–patient contact or identifiable records.

Icons & diagrams

Health Icons outline only, official 48-unit source grid, currentColor and paired English/Bangla labels. Do not mix icon families or variants. Clinical diagrams receive professional review and remain separate from brand illustration.

Motion & video

Path-drawing animation limited to introductions; 200ms interface transitions; captions, transcripts and no unverified patient imagery. No heartbeat animation or ambulance-style urgency for routine content.

05 / Verbal identity

Make the next step obvious

Patient-first

“Care starts with clear information.”

Lead with “Find a doctor,” “Plan your visit,” “Contact the hospital,” then verified services and preparation.

Student-first

“Learn medicine where knowledge meets practice.”

Explain curriculum, supervision, campus, policies and official admissions steps without implying guaranteed outcomes.

One institution

“One campus. One purpose. Healthier communities.”

Use “college and hospital” accurately; avoid suggesting every hospital service is part of every student’s training.

Message houseRoof: Growing learning. Growing wellbeing. Pillars: useful knowledge / respectful care / shared community benefit. Proof: verified faculty, supervised learning, hospital services, policies and public information.
Calls to actionSpecific verbs: Find a doctor / Check admission requirements / Download the notice / Contact the office. Avoid “Click here,” “Best care,” “World class,” or unsupported immediacy.
Bilingual ruleMatch action, dates, prices, locations and disclaimers in both languages. Never put essential Bangla information only in an image; all digital text remains selectable and screen-reader accessible.
Clinical contentQualified clinical reviewer, publication and review dates, sources, limitations and escalation guidance. Brand materials do not provide individual diagnosis or replace emergency instructions.

07 / Control & rollout

One system, accountable owners

Deliverables after selection

  • Master, entity-descriptor, responsive, reverse and single-color SVG/PDF/PNG packages.
  • Digital design tokens and reusable patient/student page templates.
  • Accessible Source Sans/Noto font and license register.
  • Journey-based wayfinding kit and bilingual signage drawings.
  • Clinical/academic content templates, consent controls and source register.

Model-specific checks

  • Does every campaign connect education and care truthfully?
  • Can each audience complete a task without decoding the organization?
  • Do entity descriptors remain subordinate to one master brand?
  • Are green/green/green/teal UI combinations accessible at the actual text size?
  • Are patient consent, privacy and clinical review documented?

Decision gate: Choose this model if SAMC’s priority is a modern, coherent college–hospital experience with strong digital services. Confirm operational responsibilities before promising “connected care,” and validate all user journeys with real audiences.
