Proposal 04 / Institutional Nexus
Care · Knowledge · Integrity

One institution.
Two missions.
One nexus.

A master identity designed around the relationship between medical care, professional education, evidence and the people SAMC serves.

01 / Strategic idea

Beyond “college” or “hospital”.

The identity should communicate an integrated academic-health institution without becoming a generic hospital, university crest, wellness brand or technology startup.

CARE

Medical Care Services

Patients, families, safety, clinical excellence and responsible service.

KNOWLEDGE

Medical Professional Education

Students, faculty, supervised practice, assessment, scholarship and professional development.

NEXUS

Clinical–Academic Integration

The controlled connection where learning, evidence and care reinforce each other.

05 / Brand behavior

One master identity. Clear descriptors.

Master institutional identity.
Used for governance, public institution, major communications and formal identity.

Clinical descriptor.

06 / Design principle

Not a collection of logos.

The strongest SAMC identity is a controlled institutional system: one master mark, one typography framework, one grid, one voice, and explicit functional descriptors.
