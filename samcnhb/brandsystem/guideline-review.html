01 / Verdict

Useful detail-level benchmark, not release-ready as supplied

The guideline has strong architecture, bilingual thinking, governance and a comprehensive 77-surface scope. Its main problems are version inconsistency, missing concrete logo/accessibility specifications, document presentation and an outdated name reference elsewhere in the workspace.

Important correction: The new supplied HD crest reads SOUTH APOLLO MEDICAL COLLEGE & HOSPITAL. The previous South Asian logo audit applied to the older logo/logo.png, not this new crest. The HTML system has been updated accordingly. This resolves the visual name discrepancy for the crest edition, but not the separate need to verify registered college/hospital names and rights.

03 / Verification still required

Not errors to invent answers for

Institutional approval

  • Current registered college and hospital wording; the combined crest name is not proof of each entity’s legal name.
  • ESTD: 2021 evidence, trademark/ownership status and authority to use/modify the crest.
  • Approved original vector and exact production colors; supplied PNGs alone are not a complete master set.
  • Named approver, date, version owner and change history.

Production and content

  • Actual minimum-size tests for print, screen, embroidery and signage.
  • Font licenses, Aptos/Bangla availability and cross-platform rendering.
  • EN/BN native-speaker review, assistive-technology testing and clinical/academic approval.
  • Whether all 77 surfaces are required, and their owners/priorities; no finished 77-asset package is implied.

The crest’s caduceus-like device and circular academic form are common motifs; their presence does not establish trademark distinctiveness, accreditation or affiliation.
