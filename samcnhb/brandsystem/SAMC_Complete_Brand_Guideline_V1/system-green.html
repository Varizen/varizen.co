02 — Typography

Muller

English master family. Use for headlines, navigation, body, labels and institutional naming. Keep hierarchy through weight and size rather than adding another typeface.

Noto Sans Bengali

Bangla master family. Use for Bangla headlines, UI, patient/student communication and bilingual materials. Keep line-height generous enough for complex Bengali shaping.

2-family ceiling

Maximum two typefaces in this system: one English family and one Bangla family. Fallbacks are technical substitutes only, not creative additions.

03 — Logo behavior

Do

  • Use approved artwork.
  • Maintain clear space.
  • Use approved positive/reverse versions.
  • Keep strong contrast.

Do not

  • Stretch or compress.
  • Recolor the master mark.
  • Add gradients, shadows or outlines.
  • Crop or mask the mark.

Descriptors

Medical Care Services and Medical Professional Education are descriptors under the master identity, not independent logos.

05 — System-specific art direction

Photography

Human, approachable, connected, optimistic. Prioritize authentic people, respectful clinical moments, learning environments and real institutional context. Keep backgrounds quiet so the message remains primary.

Illustration & iconography

Use simple outline iconography, consistent stroke weight and minimal fills. Avoid decorative icon clusters. The icon explains an action or concept; it does not compete with the logo.

Motion

Use short, restrained transitions for digital products. No ornamental logo morphing, spinning marks or constant animated backgrounds.
