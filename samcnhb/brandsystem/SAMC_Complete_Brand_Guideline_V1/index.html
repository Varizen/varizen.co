SAMC / Complete Brand Guideline / V1.0

One institution.
Three possible visual systems.

A production-ready governance framework built from the supplied brand-system package. Each system is deliberately self-contained: one color set, one English type family, one Bangla type family, one visual voice. The three systems are alternatives — not a palette to mix.

Important: this guideline applies OpenAI-style design discipline — clarity, restraint, whitespace, hierarchy, accessibility and controlled variation — but it does not reproduce OpenAI trademarks, logos, proprietary artwork or identity assets. The supplied SAMC logo remains a reference asset pending legal/name approval.

01 — Brand architecture

Master identity

SAMC is the institutional master. Division names are descriptors, not separate brands. Use the master identity consistently across Medical Care Services, Medical Professional Education, Research & Innovation and shared institutional services.

Do not create a separate logo for every department. Departmental differentiation comes from naming, information hierarchy, color/system behavior and content — not logo proliferation.

03 — Non-negotiable design rules

  1. Minimal, intentional composition: one dominant idea per surface.
  2. Generous whitespace and strong alignment; avoid decorative clutter.
  3. One accent family per brand system; do not mix the three systems on one institutional surface.
  4. No gratuitous gradients, glossy effects, texture overlays or ornamental patterns.
  5. Typography is restrained: maximum two typefaces per system — one English family and one Bangla family.
  6. Accessibility is a design requirement: maintain WCAG AA contrast for normal text and test real production combinations.
  7. The logo is an identity asset, not a decoration: do not stretch, recolor, crop, mask, outline, shadow or add effects.
  8. Photography should feel human, credible and documentary rather than stock-perfect or overly clinical.
  9. Icons should be monochrome/outlined where practical and remain subordinate to the information hierarchy.
  10. Motion should clarify hierarchy or affordance; remove motion that exists only for decoration.
  11. Co-branding requires balanced scale, protected space and approval by both parties.

05 — Color governance

One site / one system / one palette. A website, campaign, facility, event or document set chooses exactly one of the three systems. Do not place Blue Professional primary buttons beside Green Wellness primary buttons or Purple Innovation accents. Neutral black/white/gray may be used as functional neutrals.

07 — Production logic for all 231 assets

Identity: use the approved SAMC mark without modification.

Layout: use a consistent grid, generous margins and a single dominant message.

Type: use only the system's selected English/Bangla pair. Never introduce a third font inside a production asset.

Color: primary for identity/major actions; deep for high-contrast headers; light for quiet support; accent only for emphasis; support color only for controlled secondary emphasis. Never use the full swatch set merely because it exists.

Photography: real people, credible clinical/academic environments, natural light, respectful patient privacy. Avoid identifiable patient information without documented permission.

Icons: consistent outline geometry, semantic labels and adequate touch targets. The supplied package's Health Icons source is CC0; preserve provenance in the asset register.

Data: never encode meaning through color alone. Add labels, shapes or annotations.
