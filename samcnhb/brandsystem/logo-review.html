03 / Technical assessment

What still needs verification

Observed construction

  • Circular crest with institutional name, stars and ESTD: 2021.
  • Red/blue central field, open-book motif and winged staff with two serpents.
  • Blue SAMC ribbon; cyan/yellow internal graphic details.
  • Full-color, white, black and on-background HD PNG variants.

Common medical/academic devices do not prove trademark distinctiveness or institutional authorization.

Production limitations

  • Supplied package contains raster PNGs; the guideline refers to vector/master artwork that is not included here.
  • Curved lettering, thin linework and internal motifs will fail at very small sizes if not simplified by an authorized designer.
  • Do not auto-trace or reconstruct the crest for official production without rights/design review.
  • Do not crop the circular emblem and call it an approved icon-only variant without formal approval.

Required before use

Confirm registered college/hospital wording, establishment date, rights ownership, authorized vector and color specifications. Test clear space, 30mm print / 120px digital provisional minimums, embroidery and signage at actual size.

Do not

Stretch, recolor, redraw, re-typeset, extract components, use the crest as a repeating pattern, treat it as an accreditation seal, or mix the old South Asian artwork with the South Apollo edition.

04 / Relationship to concept models

Concepts remain alternatives; the crest is the reference for this edition

The three color/typography models can inform supporting layouts, but they must not invent competing institutional crests. A crest-led direction uses the supplied South Apollo mark plus controlled descriptor, typography and icon systems.

Identity hierarchy

Supplied crest → SAMC short name → division/department descriptor. Medical Care Services, Medical Professional Education and Research & Innovation are descriptors, not separate logos.

Functional icons

Use Health Icons outline only for UI and wayfinding. The crest is never reduced to a routine interface icon or replaced by an icon-family hospital symbol.
