01 / Brand idea

Knowledge that moves medicine forward

The most future-facing direction: a growing canopy framed as expanding knowledge, a continuous stem of inquiry and two outward pathways for research, technology and compassionate application.

Positioning

Future-ready medical learning

For students, educators, researchers and partners who want rigorous medical education connected to responsible digital practice and a global outlook.

Promise

Innovation with a human outcome

Technology and research are valuable when they improve learning, patient safety, accessibility and community health—not as claims of modernity alone.

Personality

Curious, precise, optimistic

Purple and indigo lead; clean geometric typography, structured data, selective pink/teal highlights and evidence-led storytelling.

Symbol logic

Canopy: expanding knowledge and possibility. Stem: continuous inquiry. Two lines: research and compassionate application. Open shelter: a learning community open to responsible progress.

Construction

100-unit geometric container with a 3-unit outline; central stem at 50 units; mirrored canopy with independent asymmetry; delta lines 2.5 units. Preserve open space between leaves and outer contour.

Distinct by emotion

A growing knowledge canopy replaces the formal shield and geometric connection mark. It is a concept for learning and discovery, not an innovation certification, global emblem or technology claim.

Crest-led alternative: The supplied HD crest reads “South Apollo Medical College & Hospital.” Use that crest—not this concept canopy—if the crest-led edition is selected. Legal/entity wording, rights and vector artwork remain verification gates. Review crest assets. Functional icons: Health Icons outline only.

Innovation lockup
“Purple Innovation” is a model theme, not an institutional renaming or innovation-center claim.

Minimum
Symbol: 10mm / 32px; simplify the two knowledge-path lines below this. Full lockup: 45mm / 250px. Proof on fabric.

Use

Purple or single-color black on white; white on deep purple; indigo/pink/teal only in controlled accessible layouts. Simplified one-color symbol for embroidery and small applications.

Do not

Turn leaves into unrelated symbols, add watermarks, claim innovation accreditation, replace the wordmark with “Purple Innovation,” use generic technology clip-art, or place detailed marks over busy photographs.

07 / Control & rollout

Innovation needs evidence and boundaries

Deliverables after selection

  • Full/simplified knowledge-growth vectors; horizontal, stacked, reverse and one-color masters.
  • Purple/indigo accessible digital tokens and physical color proofs.
  • Licensed English font plus Anek/Noto Bangla templates and font register.
  • Research, learning, presentation and data-visualization templates.
  • Technology-claim evidence, research review, consent and asset registers.

Model-specific checks

  • Is innovation expressed through actual learning, research or service capability?
  • Are research claims, data and global partnerships verified?
  • Do digital experiences meet accessibility, privacy and support requirements?
  • Are futuristic graphics excluded from safety-critical information?
  • Has the symbol passed distinctiveness, small-size and legal review?

Decision gate: Choose Purple Innovation only if SAMC has or will fund a real digital-learning/research roadmap. Otherwise Blue Professional is the stronger institutional default. “Future-ready” must describe a substantiated strategy, not an invented ranking or outcome.
