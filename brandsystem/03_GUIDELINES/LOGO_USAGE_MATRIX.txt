VARIZEN LOGO USAGE MATRIX

LIGHT BACKGROUND
- Desktop navbar/header: Primary Horizontal Color
- Footer: Primary Horizontal Color
- Dashboard/cards/pricing: Primary Horizontal Color
- Formal monochrome document: Monochrome Horizontal Black
- Centered/mobile/splash: Primary Stacked Color

DARK BACKGROUND
- Desktop navbar/header: Reverse Horizontal White
- Footer: Reverse Horizontal White
- Dark hero/video/image overlay: Reverse Horizontal White
- Centered hero/mobile/splash: Reverse Stacked White

ICON-ONLY
- Favicon/app icon: symbol-only V mark, transparent background
- Do not use the full wordmark at favicon sizes

NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Keep >= 0.5x logo-height clear space
- Never distort, rotate, skew or redraw the mark
- Maintain optical alignment
- Preserve transparency when possible
