## 1. Four Governed Experience Segments

2. **Segment 02 — Service & Operations Center:** Operational SOPs, incident runbooks, disaster relief dispatch protocols, physical field checklists.
3. **Segment 03 — Administration & Governance Center:** Board procedure manuals, statutory filing runbooks, IAM delegation rules, regulatory change administration.
4. **Segment 04 — Moderation & Relief Center:** Governed triage queues for Moderator A and Moderator B, operational relief procedures, and strict boundary monitoring.
