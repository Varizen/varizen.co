## 2. Evaluation Outcomes & Human Escalation
- **Outcomes:** `CLEAR`, `ADVISORY`, `WARNING`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `HOLD`, `BLOCKED`.
- **Legal Hold Mandate:** An action placed on `HOLD` or `BLOCKED` cannot be cleared by AI agents or a single operator. Clearance requires formal Legal Review Case resolution and dual-authority sign-off.
