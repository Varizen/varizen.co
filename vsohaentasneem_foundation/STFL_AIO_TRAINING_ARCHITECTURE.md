## 1. Six-Stage Training Lifecycle

Every training curriculum follows a rigorous pedagogical sequence:
```
OVERVIEW -> VISUAL FLOW -> STEP-BY-STEP -> PRACTICE -> ASSESSMENT -> CERTIFICATION
```

- **Versioned Provenance:** Training modules are versioned, dated, and approved by the Training Lead.
- **Cryptographic Certificate Issuance:** Upon successful assessment completion (score >= 80%), a verified `STFL-CER-*` credential is created with public verification hash.
