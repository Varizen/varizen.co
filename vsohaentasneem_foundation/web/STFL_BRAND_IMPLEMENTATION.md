# STFL /brand — Implementation Brief

Refine the existing `/brand` page.

DO NOT rebuild the entire website.

DO NOT change Android.

DO NOT change the backend.

DO NOT replace the STFL logo.

DO NOT copy OpenAI.

The desired direction is an editorial brand-guideline experience with
high visual discipline.

## Required visual behavior

### Header

Quiet, minimal header.

Use generous horizontal padding.

Brand mark should never compete with page content.

### Hero

Large whitespace.

Small section label.

Strong title:

SohaelTasneem Foundation

Supporting line:

Building better futures.

Avoid oversized marketing graphics.

### Navigation

Use a simple section index.

Prefer:

01 Foundation
02 Logo
03 Clear Space
04 Sizing
05 Misuse
06 Color
07 Typography
08 Digital Language
09 Applications
10 Partnerships
11 Governance

### Content

Each section should have:

- section number
- concise title
- short explanatory paragraph
- visual specimen where useful
- clear spacing

### Logo

Use only the supplied native STFL logo assets.

Show:

- primary
- black
- white
- full-color
- horizontal
- vertical

Never distort.

### Clear Space

Create a simple diagram showing the minimum protected area.

Do not invent a numerical clear-space measurement unless the existing
STFL brand specification defines one.

If no official measurement exists, label the rule as a visual principle
rather than fabricating a numeric standard.

### Sizing

Show practical digital examples:

- favicon
- navigation
- website header
- social profile
- document
- large-format

Do not claim minimum sizes unless officially established.

### Misuse

Show concise examples:

DO NOT stretch
DO NOT rotate
DO NOT crop
DO NOT recolor
DO NOT add effects
DO NOT place over busy images

### Color

Use large, quiet swatches.

Prioritize:

STFL Ink
STFL Deep Navy
STFL Snow
STFL Stone

Use STFL Accent sparingly.

### Typography

Hind Siliguri for Bangla.

Inter/system sans for English if already used by the application.

Demonstrate hierarchy rather than decorative typography.

### Digital Language

Describe STFL's voice:

clear
human
calm
credible
inclusive
precise

### Partnerships

Show how STFL's identity sits beside another logo without implying
endorsement.

Use balanced spacing and hierarchy.

### Footer

Minimal.

Use:

SohaelTasneem Foundation © 2001–2026

Do not write:

SohaelTasneem Foundation

## Motion

Keep animation subtle.

No decorative motion.

## Accessibility

Maintain semantic headings, focus states, keyboard access and reduced
motion.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop:

large whitespace and strong editorial grid.

Mobile:

single-column flow with generous but efficient spacing.

No horizontal overflow.

## Overall aesthetic

Think:

institutional editorial publication
+
modern technology design discipline
+
humanitarian warmth

Not:

startup landing page
Not:
generic AI website
Not:
template marketplace
Not:
OpenAI clone
