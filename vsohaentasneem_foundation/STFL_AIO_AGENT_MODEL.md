## 1. Invariant 1: Level 3 Authority Ceiling

AI Agents operate under Class 10 identity (`CLASS_10`). Their capabilities are strictly bounded:
- **Agents MAY:** Monitor, calculate, classify, detect anomalies, summarize records, draft documents, recommend approvals, and orchestrate automated scheduled jobs.
- **Agents CANNOT:** Grant themselves permissions, approve their own recommendations, waive compliance holds, disburse high-risk funds, or alter constitutional rules.
