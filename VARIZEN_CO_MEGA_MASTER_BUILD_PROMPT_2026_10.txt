0. MISSION
====================================================================

Build varizen.co as the authoritative public digital headquarters of Varizen Inc.

This is NOT a generic SaaS landing page, template, startup mockup, or cybersecurity-themed brochure.

It must function simultaneously as:

1. A premium corporate website.
2. A clear explanation of what Varizen actually builds.
3. A trustworthy cybersecurity / digital-trust presence.
4. A governed-AI and cloud-platform company surface.
5. A portfolio gateway for Varizen ventures and products.
6. A public entry point into the controlled Showcase and Investment ecosystems.
7. A Trust Center and evidence-oriented corporate surface.
8. A production-grade responsive web application foundation.
9. A future authenticated application shell without exposing protected information.
10. A technical expression of Varizen's operating philosophy: many isolated systems, one governed experience.

The final experience should feel like a serious global technology company:
Apple-level product discipline + OpenAI/Vercel/Linear/Stripe-level clarity + enterprise security credibility.

Do NOT copy another company's visual identity.
Use those companies only as quality references for hierarchy, restraint, typography, interaction quality, and information architecture.

Core positioning:

"Digital trust, made operational."

Supporting idea:

"Know your exposure. Prove your protection."

Varizen should communicate that it turns cybersecurity, privacy, AI governance, cloud operations, evidence, and operational uncertainty into measurable, governed action.

1. IMPORTANT SOURCE-OF-TRUTH RULE
====================================================================

Before writing UI code:

AUDIT ALL AVAILABLE VARIZEN ASSETS.

The existing Varizen logo assets in the project/library are authoritative.
Do not replace the native Varizen identity with a newly invented logo.

Known existing logo variants include:
- Varizen white side-by-side
- Varizen white vertical/up-and-down
- Varizen original/colored versions
- Varizen OG vertical version
- symbol-only V mark assets where available

Use the best available source-quality asset.

Logo rules:
- Never stretch.
- Never squash.
- Never rotate.
- Never skew.
- Never redraw unnecessarily.
- Never alter proportions.
- Preserve native identity.
- Preserve optical spacing.
- Use transparent SVG/PNG/WebP where appropriate.
- Prefer SVG when a verified vector exists.
- Maintain at least 0.5x logo-height clear space.
- Use side-by-side for desktop navigation/footer.
- Use vertical/up-down for mobile, centered/splash contexts, and compact applications.
- Use white on dark backgrounds.
- Use original/colored on light backgrounds.
- Use symbol-only V for favicon/app icon.
- Provide retina-ready assets.
- Alt text must be "Varizen logo" unless context requires more specificity.

If an asset is low quality:
- clean it,
- remove accidental background,
- sharpen,
- vectorize only when necessary,
- preserve the exact geometry.

Do not fabricate a new corporate mark.
